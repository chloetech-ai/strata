Cluster · 1M passages · laptop · 2 November 2026

Fourteen engines on one laptop's cluster

A kind cluster inside Docker Desktop on a MacBook Air, 3 CPUs and 6 GiB per engine. The nodes share four CPUs, so this compares the engines with each other, not with real machines. BEIR NQ's judgments score every answer with nDCG@10.

On this small budget ChloeDB is fastest on 14 of 45 tests, in the top half on 40 and never last, and ties for first on BM25 nDCG@10. It is behind on typo search, SQL aggregates and key lookups. One pass.

Machine

Cluster

Budget
