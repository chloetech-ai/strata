Features

One engine for the database, the search and the ranking

Each table is indexed four ways at once over the same rows: posting lists for full text, a columnar forward index for structured fields, vector indexes, and graph edges. A cost-based planner chooses, per segment and from exact statistics, how to run each part of a statement.

Full text

posting lists, BM25

BM25 and boolean queries

shipped

MATCH takes AND, OR, NOT, phrases and groups; bm25() scores. MaxScore and Block-Max WAND skip what cannot reach the top k.

MATCH

Weighted, required and excluded words

shipped

Word weights, required and excluded words, "x of y" word groups, and AT_LEAST(n, …) over any conditions.

Autocomplete that tolerates typos

shipped

PREFIX(kw, :typed, fuzzy => 'auto') walks the sorted dictionary as a trie, so its cost follows the values near what was typed.

Prefixes

Posting lists from our own study

shipped

Loom and BP128 posting lists. Loom is the size of partitioned Elias-Fano, with 2.4–3.0× faster AND and 3.8–7.7× faster OR.

Posting lists

Analyzers by name and version

shipped

english stems and drops stopwords, simple keeps every word. A field records its analyzer, so a version that analyses differently refuses it rather than misread it.

Vectors

HNSW, IVF, 8-bit codes

HNSW, IVF or exact

shipped

float32, 8-bit or binary codes. HNSW graphs build on 8-bit codes, and merges join their inputs' graphs instead of starting over.

Vector indexes

Filtered search, planned

shipped

Per segment, from exact counts: an exact scan of the matches, the filter inside the HNSW walk, or a post-filter that widens the walk. 1.8–6.1× faster than the best fixed strategy.

The planner

Hybrid fusion

shipped

rrf(), and minmax(), tmm() or zscore() in a weighted sum, over BM25, vectors, graphs and columns, under the same filter as their inputs.

Fusion

Multi-vector MaxSim

Query language

DataFusion for compound plans

in progress

Apache DataFusion runs joins and aggregates over Strata's index scans (1M-row joins 2.2–3.8× faster); unions and window functions next.

ACID with snapshot isolation

shipped

Shadow-paged commits and a write-ahead log. Readers never wait for the writer; every crash test recovered a committed prefix.
