Completion, plans and charts. Read-only on production until someone allows writes.

Change

Change the cluster safely

Rollouts pick a published version and restart one copy at a time behind health gates. Autoscaling rules read as sentences. Cloud setup prices and creates a cluster on EKS or DigitalOcean. All clusters puts every cluster you reach on one page, and Follow keeps one a moment behind another.

What the others ship for this

Elasticsearch and OpenSearch leave the console to a second product, Kibana or OpenSearch Dashboards. Milvus has Attu as a separate service.
