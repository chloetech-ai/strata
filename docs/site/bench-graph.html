Graph · arXiv citations · MacBook Air · 3 November 2026

Graphs in your tables, against four graph databases

ChloeDB keeps edges in a table's rows, or in an edge table, and walks them inside SQL. Both layouts ran against Neo4j, Memgraph, FalkorDB and ArangoDB on a citation graph of 169,343 papers and 1.17M citations. Every engine's answers matched an exact reference.

ChloeDB's two layouts place first and second over 16 tests: 31 and 46 points, then Memgraph 60 and Neo4j 67. Without PageRank, which only ChloeDB and Neo4j run, it is close: ChloeDB 21, Memgraph 24.

Overall

Summed places over 16 tests

Median and 95th percentile on six kinds, PageRank recall, load time and memory. Each ChloeDB layout is ranked on its own; a test a setup cannot run counts as last.

Latency

Short hops: the in-memory stores. Paths and PageRank: ChloeDB

Memgraph and FalkorDB answer one to three hops in about half ChloeDB's time. ChloeDB is fastest on shortest paths, and on two-hop expansion at the 95th percentile. Its PageRank takes 1.42 ms against Neo4j's 93.1, as an approximation (below). ChloeDB is shown over gRPC and over HTTP JSON: gRPC takes about 0.1 ms off its short hops. On one hop the rest of its gap is its own time, 0.10 ms against Memgraph's 0.02, and the Python client's gRPC call, 0.25 ms against 0.17 for Bolt.

How exact

PageRank trades exactness for speed

ChloeDB pushes from the seed's neighbourhood to a tolerance; Neo4j iterates over the whole graph. Neo4j is closer to the exact top 20 at every setting: ChloeDB's finest gets 0.955 in 52.6 ms, Neo4j's coarsest 0.988 in 93.1 ms.

Load and footprint

Loaded in about a second, with the least memory

The field layout writes one row a paper and keeps 68 MB on disk; FalkorDB, all in memory, saved nothing during the run. As one row a citation, like the others, ChloeDB loads in 6.22 s against Memgraph's 6.96.
