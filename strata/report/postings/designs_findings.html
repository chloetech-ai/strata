On 8 of the 10 size/time frontiers (AND and OR on five workloads), every point is Pyramid, Loom or Entropic. Roaring keeps the fast end of AND on uniform and WikiText.

Loom vs PEF
same size, 2× AND

0.4–0.9% smaller, with 1.9–2.2× faster conjunctions and 4.9–8.9× faster unions. On clustered data it also beats Roaring on both operations at 53% of its size.

Pyramid vs Roaring
24–33% smaller

The fastest union on every workload (1.2–1.75× Roaring). Conjunctions are faster on clustered data, but take 11% more time on uniform and 38% more on WikiText.

Entropic
6–15% below PEF

The smallest serialized index on every collection, and 2.2–2.4× faster on AND than interpolative coding. Decode tables add 1.3–22 MB of memory, and builds take 13–19× longer than PEF's.
